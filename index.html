Guidelines

Usage Rules

Keep the logo clean, consistent, and respectful of its organic identity.

Minimum Size

The full emblem must not appear smaller than 24px in diameter. For small spaces, use the leaf icon alone.

Clear Space

Maintain a clear zone equal to the radius of the inner circle on all sides. No overlapping elements.

Colour Fidelity

Use only approved palette colours. On kraft paper, switch to the single-colour version for print clarity.

No Alterations

Do not rotate, skew, stretch, or add effects (shadows, outlines, gradients) to the logo mark.

Background Rules

On dark backgrounds use the gold/sage version. On light backgrounds use the full-colour earth/green version.

Lock-up Only

The arched text "KABO" and "FARMING" must always appear together with the emblem. Never separate them.
